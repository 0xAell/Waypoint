# Waypoint

Songs carried across languages.

A song stays in the language it was written in. These lyric videos put a second language beside it: the original words and a translation on the same screen, each word lit as it is sung. Most are Russian songs with English beside them. A few go the other way, or start in French, Spanish or Japanese.

The videos are on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@0xAell). This repository is the index: who made each song, which languages it moves between, where it came from and where to watch it.

The music, words, performances and artwork belong to the artists credited here and to their rights holders. The translation, the timing and the lyric picture around them are the only parts made for these videos. No lyrics, audio, video or artwork are kept in this repository.

## How they are made

Translate for meaning, time every word against the original recording, then render both languages at equal weight over the original picture or a new one. Translation, timing and animation are made with AI assistance, then directed and reviewed by a person. The longer version is in [how it's made](docs/how-its-made.md).

## Credits and corrections

Video descriptions credit the original artist and link the original recording. If a credit here is wrong or incomplete, open an issue with the source and it will be corrected.
